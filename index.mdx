## Get Started in 3 Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your account">
    Sign up at [jarvis-ai.app](https://jarvis-ai.app) and verify your email to activate your Jarvis interface.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the HUD">
    Once logged in, your Jarvis HUD launches automatically. The chat panel and voice activator are ready immediately.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Say hello">
    Type a message or click the microphone to start speaking. Jarvis will greet you and await your command.
  </Step>
</Steps>
