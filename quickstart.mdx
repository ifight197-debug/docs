# Get Started with Jarvis AI

You're moments away from your first conversation with the most capable AI assistant you've ever used. Follow these steps and you'll be up and running before your coffee gets cold.

<Note>
  No credit card is required to create a free Jarvis account. You get full access to core chat and voice features from day one.
</Note>

---

<Steps>

  <Step title="Create Your Account">
    Head to [jarvis-ai.app](https://jarvis-ai.app) and click **Get Started Free** on the homepage.

    You can sign up using:
    - 📧 **Email** — Enter your email address and choose a password
    - 🍎 **Apple** — Sign in with your Apple ID in one tap
    - 🔵 **Google** — Use your Google account for instant access

    All sign-up methods are equally fast. Pick whichever you trust most.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Verify Your Email and Log In">
    If you signed up with email, check your inbox for a verification message from **hello@jarvis-ai.app**. Click the **Confirm My Account** button inside.

    > If you used Google or Apple sign-in, email verification is handled automatically — skip straight to logging in.

    Once verified, return to [jarvis-ai.app](https://jarvis-ai.app) and sign in with your credentials. You'll land directly in the Jarvis HUD.
  </Step>

  <Step title="The HUD Loads — Here's What You're Seeing">
    Welcome to the Jarvis heads-up display. The interface assembles itself with a satisfying boot animation, then settles into your workspace. Here's a quick orientation:

    | Element | Location | What It Does |
    |---|---|---|
    | **Chat Panel** | Left / Center | Where you type your messages to Jarvis |
    | **Response Panel** | Right / Center | Where Jarvis's replies appear, with animated text |
    | **Voice Activator** | Bottom Center | The glowing circular button — your gateway to voice mode |
    | **Status Indicator** | Top Bar | Lights showing whether Jarvis is ready, processing, or listening |
    | **Settings** | Top Right (⚙️) | Access personality, voice, and HUD theme options |

    Take a moment to look around. The glowing arcs and grid lines aren't just decoration — every element is functional.

    <Tip>
      Grant microphone permissions now, before you need them. Click the **Voice Activator** button and accept the browser prompt. This saves you a disruption mid-conversation later.
    </Tip>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Send Your First Message">
    Click inside the **Chat Panel** (the text input at the bottom of the left/center pane). Type your first message and press **Enter** to send.

    Try something like:

    ```text
    Hello Jarvis, what can you do?
    ```

    Jarvis will respond with a summary of its capabilities — a great way to discover features you might want to explore next. The reply animates into the Response Panel character by character, just like a real-time transmission from your AI co-pilot.

    Other great first messages:
    ```text
    Give me a quick summary of the latest advances in AI.
    ```
    ```text
    Help me write a professional out-of-office email.
    ```
    ```text
    Explain recursion like I'm a smart ten-year-old.
    ```
  </Step>

  <Step title="Switch to Voice Mode (Optional)">
    Ready to go hands-free? Voice mode is one click away.

    1. Click the **circular microphone button** at the bottom center of the HUD — it will glow and pulse to confirm it's active.
    2. If prompted, click **Allow** in your browser's microphone permission dialog.
    3. Speak your message naturally — no need to use special commands or keywords.
    4. When you're done speaking, either pause for a moment (Jarvis detects silence automatically) or click the microphone button again to stop.

    ```text
    "Jarvis, what's the capital of Iceland, and what's it known for?"
    ```

    Jarvis listens, processes, and responds — both as animated text in the HUD and (optionally) as synthesized speech.

    <Tip>
      For the best voice experience, use Jarvis in a quiet environment with a headset or a device with a quality microphone. Background noise can affect transcription accuracy.
    </Tip>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Jarvis Replies — Watch the HUD Come Alive">
    Whether you typed or spoke your message, Jarvis's response arrives with full visual flair:

    - **Animated text** streams into the Response Panel in real time
    - **Status lights** shift from orange (processing) to green (ready) as the response completes
    - **Markdown formatting** renders automatically — headings, bullet points, bold text, and code blocks all display cleanly
    - In voice mode, a **waveform animation** pulses as Jarvis speaks its reply back to you

    That's it — you're having a conversation with Jarvis. From here, the only limit is what you ask.
  </Step>

</Steps>
