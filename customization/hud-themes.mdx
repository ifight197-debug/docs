The Jarvis HUD isn't just functional — it's an experience. From the warm glow of the original Stark Industries red-and-gold to the cold precision of a cyberpunk neon grid, HUD Themes let you dress your interface to match your mission. All visual customisation options live in **Settings → Appearance**.

## Accessing Appearance Settings

Navigate to **Settings (⚙️) → Appearance** tab to access all theme and visual customisation options. Changes apply in real-time as you adjust — no save or reload required.

<Note>
  Theme changes are applied **instantly as you make adjustments** — you'll see the HUD update live behind the settings panel. There is no "save" step for visual settings; simply close the panel when you're happy with the result.
</Note>

## Built-In Themes

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="🔴 Iron Man Classic (Default)">
    **Palette:** Red, gold, and gunmetal grey.

    The original Stark Industries colour scheme. Bold, iconic, and immediately recognisable — this is the default Jarvis experience. Red primary panels, gold accent lines, and deep grey backgrounds echo the Mark III armour. If you're not sure where to start, start here.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="🔵 Arc Reactor Blue">
    **Palette:** Electric blue and crisp white.

    Inspired by the arc reactor's cold fusion glow, this theme bathes the HUD in brilliant blue light with bright white text and circuit-line accents. High contrast and easy on the eyes in darkened environments. A favourite for late-night sessions.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="🟠 Stealth">
    **Palette:** Dark charcoal and burnt orange.

    Low-light. Tactical. No unnecessary brightness. The Stealth theme strips the interface back to near-black backgrounds with subtle orange outlines — designed to reduce eye strain and minimise screen glow in dark rooms. Think field operations, not press conferences.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="🟣 Vibranium">
    **Palette:** Deep purple and metallic silver.

    Wakandan-inspired, this theme channels the energy of vibranium — deep violet backgrounds shimmer with silver filigree accents and a subtle iridescent sheen on panel borders. Elegant, powerful, and unlike anything from Stark's wardrobe.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="⬜ Ghost">
    **Palette:** Clean white and light grey.

    Jarvis's light mode. Ghost reduces the HUD to its essentials — a minimal white interface with soft grey dividers and barely-there animations. Ideal for well-lit spaces, presentations, or users who prefer a cleaner aesthetic over the full sci-fi experience.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="🟢 Neon Circuit">
    **Palette:** Bright green on pure black.

    Full cyberpunk. Neon Circuit renders the HUD as a glowing green circuit board against a black void, with pulsing grid lines and high-energy particle effects. Maximalist, retro-futuristic, and undeniably cool — if your setup has RGB lighting, this is the theme to match it to.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Accent Color

Every built-in theme has a default accent color, but you can override it with any custom hex value using the **Accent Color Picker**.

Click the color swatch next to **Accent Color** in the Appearance tab, then either:
- Pick from the color wheel, or
- Type a hex value directly (e.g. `#00FFD1` for a mint green accent)

The accent color applies to interactive elements, active borders, progress indicators, and highlighted text throughout the HUD.

## Background Intensity

<ParamField name="Background Intensity" type="slider" default="60%">
  Controls the brightness of the animated HUD background effects — glowing grids, reactive panels, and ambient light pulses. 

  - **Low (0–30%)**: Subtle, unobtrusive ambience.
  - **Medium (30–70%)**: Balanced visual presence without distraction.
  - **High (70–100%)**: Full cinematic immersion.
</ParamField>

---

## Particle Density

<ParamField name="Particle Density" type="select" default="Medium">
  Determines how many animated particles, floating sparks, and data fragments drift across the HUD background.

  | Option | Description |
  |---|---|
  | **Off** | No particles — static background only. Best for performance on lower-end hardware. |
  | **Low** | A sparse scattering of slow-moving particles. |
  | **Medium** | The default — a lively but readable particle field. |
  | **High** | Dense, fast-moving particle streams for maximum atmosphere. |
</ParamField>

---

## Reduced Motion

Toggle **Reduced Motion** to **On** to disable all HUD animations — particle effects, panel transitions, background pulses, and reactive elements will be replaced with static equivalents.

<Tip>
  **Reduced Motion** is not just an accessibility feature — it's also a meaningful performance boost on older hardware or when running Jarvis alongside resource-intensive applications. If you notice any lag or frame drops in the HUD, enabling Reduced Motion is the first thing to try.
</Tip>

---

## Compact Mode

Toggle **Compact Mode** to **On** to collapse decorative HUD elements — including corner brackets, animated borders, status readouts, and background panel flourishes — giving you a cleaner, roomier chat interface.

Compact Mode preserves all functional elements (chat input, messages, controls) while removing purely decorative chrome. Ideal for smaller screens or users who prioritise readable content over visual spectacle.
