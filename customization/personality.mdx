Jarvis is more than an assistant — it's a presence. Whether you need a no-nonsense professional briefing your morning schedule or the full Tony Stark experience complete with dry wit and "Sir", Jarvis adapts to fit the way you work and communicate. Personality settings give you precise control over tone, response length, and even how Jarvis addresses you.

## Accessing Personality Settings

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings">
    Click the **gear icon** (⚙️) in the top-right corner of the Jarvis HUD to open the Settings panel.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Navigate to Personality">
    Select the **Personality** tab from the left-hand settings menu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose your preferences">
    Adjust tone, verbosity, custom prompt, and name settings as described below.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save changes">
    Click **Apply** to save your personality configuration. Changes take effect immediately in your current session.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Tone Options

The **Tone** setting controls the overall character of Jarvis's communication style. Select the mode that best matches your preferences.

### Formal
Precise, structured, and strictly professional. Jarvis omits small talk, uses complete sentences, and keeps responses factual and direct. Ideal for work environments where efficiency matters most.

### Balanced *(Default)*
Friendly but professional — think of a highly capable colleague who knows when to be warm and when to get to the point. Balanced tone is suitable for most everyday use cases and is the recommended starting point.

### Casual
Relaxed and conversational. Jarvis uses contractions, shorter sentences, and a lighter touch. Great for personal use, brainstorming sessions, or whenever you just want a chat.

### Jarvis Mode
The full Iron Man experience. In this mode, Jarvis embodies the iconic JARVIS character from the MCU — addressing you as **"Sir"** or **"Ma'am"**, delivering responses with polished composure and dry wit, and occasionally slipping in a quip worthy of Stark Tower. 

<Tip>
  **Jarvis Mode** is more than a novelty — many users find the heightened formality paired with subtle humour makes long working sessions genuinely more enjoyable. Try it for a week before switching back.
</Tip>

## Verbosity

The **Verbosity** slider controls how long and detailed Jarvis's responses are, regardless of tone.

| Setting | Behaviour |
|---|---|
| **Concise** | Short, punchy answers. Bullet points where appropriate. Minimal elaboration. |
| **Balanced** | Default length — enough context to be useful without padding. |
| **Detailed** | Full explanations, background context, and step-by-step breakdowns where relevant. |

Drag the slider between **Concise** and **Detailed** to find your preferred level. The verbosity setting works in combination with tone — a Casual + Detailed Jarvis will give you warm, thorough answers, while Formal + Concise produces tight, structured briefs.

## Custom System Prompt

Advanced users can write a fully custom persona description to override or extend the built-in tone modes.

Navigate to **Settings → Personality → Custom System Prompt** and enter your instructions in the text box. For example:

```
You are a sharp, slightly sardonic assistant who communicates like a seasoned detective. 
Always reference the user by their last name. Favour brevity, but never sacrifice precision.
```

<Note>
  Custom system prompts override the **Tone** setting. The Verbosity slider and Name settings remain active alongside your custom prompt.
</Note>

## Name & Greeting

Personalise how Jarvis addresses you and what it says when you first open the HUD.

- **Your name / title**: Enter what you'd like Jarvis to call you — your first name, a title, a callsign, or leave it blank to have Jarvis omit direct address entirely.
- **Welcome message**: Customise the greeting Jarvis delivers when the HUD launches. You can include the time, your name, or a motivational line — or keep it minimal.

**Example custom greeting:**
> *"Welcome back, Commander. All systems nominal. Ready when you are."*
