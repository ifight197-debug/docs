# Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions? We have answers. Browse the sections below to find everything you need to know about Jarvis AI — from getting started to advanced features and billing.

## General

<AccordionGroup>

<Accordion title="What is Jarvis AI?">
  Jarvis AI is a superintelligent AI assistant inspired by the iconic AI from Iron Man, built for people who demand more than a chatbot. It combines a cutting-edge large language model with a futuristic heads-up display (HUD) interface, real-time voice conversation, and deep personalisation — giving you an assistant that feels less like software and more like a co-pilot. Whether you need help writing, coding, researching, or thinking through complex problems, Jarvis is engineered to keep up.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How is Jarvis different from other AI assistants?">
  Most AI assistants feel like talking to a search bar. Jarvis is different in three key ways: **interface**, **intelligence**, and **personality**. The signature HUD brings a sci-fi aesthetic with animated waveforms, reactive particle effects, and a dark-glass design that makes every interaction feel immersive. Under the hood, Jarvis runs on a state-of-the-art model fine-tuned for nuanced, context-aware conversation. And unlike generic assistants, Jarvis can be customised — from its communication style and voice to how it addresses you — so it adapts to *you*, not the other way around. Learn more on the [Core Features](/features/chat) page.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title='What is "Jarvis Mode" and how do I enable it?'>
  Jarvis Mode is the full-immersion experience — it activates the complete HUD overlay, ambient sound design, animated response sequences, and enhanced personality settings that make Jarvis feel truly alive. To enable it, open **Settings → Interface → Jarvis Mode** and toggle it on. You can also trigger it instantly from the chat window using the command `/jarvis-mode on`.

  <Tip>
    Jarvis Mode looks best on larger screens and works especially well paired with a dark OS theme. If you're on a lower-powered device, check the [HUD themes settings](/customization/hud-themes) to tune particle density and animation intensity for a smoother experience.
  </Tip>
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Is there a free plan?">
  Yes! Jarvis AI offers a **Free tier** that gives you access to text chat, a limited number of daily messages, and a preview of the HUD interface. Voice mode, unlimited messaging, memory across sessions, and advanced customisation options are available on **Jarvis Pro** and **Jarvis Ultra** plans. You can start for free at [jarvis-ai.app](https://jarvis-ai.app) — no credit card required.
</Accordion>

</AccordionGroup>

## Chat & Intelligence

<AccordionGroup>

<Accordion title="How smart is Jarvis? What AI model powers it?">
  Jarvis is powered by a frontier-class large language model fine-tuned specifically for Jarvis AI's tone, capabilities, and safety profile. It excels at nuanced reasoning, long-form writing, coding across dozens of languages, data analysis, and multi-step problem-solving. Pro and Ultra subscribers get access to the most capable model tier, while Free users are served by a highly capable base model. Jarvis is continuously updated as underlying model improvements are rolled out — you always have access to the latest version without needing to update anything manually.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can Jarvis remember things from previous sessions?">
  **Persistent Memory** is a Pro and Ultra feature that lets Jarvis retain key facts, preferences, and context across separate conversations. When enabled, Jarvis can recall details like your name, ongoing projects, communication preferences, and frequently used tools — so you never have to repeat yourself. You can view, edit, or delete everything Jarvis has stored by visiting **Settings → Memory**. Free plan users get in-session memory only, meaning Jarvis remembers the current conversation but starts fresh each new session.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Does Jarvis have a knowledge cutoff date?">
  Jarvis's core training data has a knowledge cutoff, meaning it may not be aware of very recent events or newly released information. However, Jarvis Pro and Ultra subscribers can enable **Live Web Access**, which allows Jarvis to search the web in real time and surface up-to-date information during your conversation. When Jarvis uses web data, it will clearly indicate the source so you can verify it. Check the [Intelligence features page](/features/intelligence) for details on how Jarvis handles current events.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can Jarvis write and debug code?">
  Absolutely — coding is one of Jarvis's strongest capabilities. It can write, review, explain, refactor, and debug code across a wide range of languages including Python, JavaScript/TypeScript, Rust, Go, C++, SQL, and many more. You can paste in broken code and ask Jarvis to find the bug, describe what you're trying to build and get a full implementation, or ask for a line-by-line explanation of unfamiliar code. Responses include syntax-highlighted code blocks for readability, and Jarvis will ask clarifying questions when a request is ambiguous.
</Accordion>

</AccordionGroup>

## Voice

<AccordionGroup>

<Accordion title="How do I use voice mode?">
  Voice mode lets you have a real-time spoken conversation with Jarvis, complete with animated waveform feedback in the HUD. To start a voice session, click the **microphone icon** in the chat toolbar (or press `V` as a keyboard shortcut). Your browser will prompt you to grant microphone permission the first time — click **Allow**. Once active, simply speak naturally and Jarvis will respond with synthesised voice output. You can switch back to text at any time by clicking the microphone icon again. For a full walkthrough, see the [Voice Mode guide](/features/voice).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Why isn't Jarvis recognising my voice?">
  Voice recognition issues are usually caused by one of a few things: microphone permissions haven't been granted, the wrong input device is selected, or there's significant background noise interfering with detection. Start by checking that your browser has microphone access (see **Settings → Privacy** in your browser), then confirm the correct microphone is selected under **Jarvis Settings → Voice → Input Device**. Speaking closer to your microphone and reducing background noise can also dramatically improve accuracy. If you're still having trouble, visit the [Troubleshooting guide](/help/troubleshooting) for step-by-step diagnostics.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I change the voice Jarvis uses to speak?">
  Yes — Jarvis offers several distinct voice profiles to suit your taste. Navigate to **Settings → Voice → Output Voice** to browse and preview available voices. Options range from deep and authoritative to calm and conversational. Pro and Ultra subscribers unlock additional premium voice profiles. You can also adjust speech rate and volume from the same settings panel. More voice customisation options, including tone and pacing fine-tuning, are covered in the [Personalisation guide](/customization/personality).

  <Note>
    Voice output quality may vary slightly depending on your device's audio hardware and the selected playback output. For the best experience, use headphones or a high-quality speaker.
  </Note>
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Does voice mode work on mobile?">
  Yes, voice mode is fully supported on mobile devices through the Jarvis AI mobile app (iOS and Android) and through mobile browsers. On mobile, tap the microphone icon in the chat interface to begin a voice session. We recommend using the native app for the most reliable experience, as browser-based microphone access on mobile can occasionally be restricted by device-level permissions. Make sure Jarvis has microphone access granted in your phone's **Settings → Apps → Jarvis** permissions panel.
</Accordion>

</AccordionGroup>

## Privacy & Data

<AccordionGroup>

<Accordion title="Does Jarvis store my conversations?">
  By default, Jarvis stores your conversation history so you can revisit past chats and so persistent memory can function on Pro and Ultra plans. Your data is encrypted at rest and in transit, and is never shared with third parties for advertising or analytics purposes. If you prefer not to have your conversations stored, you can enable **Incognito Mode** under **Settings → Privacy**, which disables history logging and memory for that session. You can also delete your full conversation history at any time from **Settings → Data → Delete All Conversations**.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Is my data used to train the AI model?">
  Jarvis AI does **not** use your personal conversations to train the underlying AI model by default. Your chats remain private to you. We may use anonymised, aggregated interaction signals (such as thumbs-up/down ratings you explicitly provide) to inform product improvements, but this is done only with clearly opt-in data. You can review our full data practices at [jarvis-ai.app/privacy](https://jarvis-ai.app/privacy) and adjust your data-sharing preferences under **Settings → Privacy → Data & Research**.
</Accordion>

</AccordionGroup>

## Account & Billing

<AccordionGroup>

<Accordion title="How do I reset my password?">
  To reset your password, click **Forgot password?** on the login screen and enter the email address associated with your account. You'll receive a password reset link within a few minutes — be sure to check your spam folder if it doesn't arrive. The reset link expires after 30 minutes for security reasons. If you're already logged in and want to change your password proactively, go to **Settings → Account → Change Password**. If you continue to have trouble accessing your account, contact [support@jarvis-ai.com](mailto:support@jarvis-ai.com).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I use Jarvis on multiple devices?">
  Yes — your Jarvis account works across all your devices. Simply log in with the same credentials on any device and your conversation history, settings, and memory (on Pro/Ultra) will sync automatically. There's no limit to the number of devices you can be logged in to simultaneously. The Jarvis AI app is available on **iOS**, **Android**, and as a **web app** that runs in any modern browser. Desktop apps for macOS and Windows are currently in beta — join the waitlist at [jarvis-ai.app](https://jarvis-ai.app).
</Accordion>

</AccordionGroup>
